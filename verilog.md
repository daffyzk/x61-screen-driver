First, get an FPGA with high throughput transciever, like the Tang Mega 138K or 60K

Develop driver code on that, then get a GW5AT-LV15MG132C1/I0 for the final PCB.

GW5AT-LV15 = Arora V 15K Logic Elements with Transceivers.
MG132 = The 8x8mm Micro-BGA package.
C1/I0 = The temperature grade (Commercial/Industrial) and speed grade.
